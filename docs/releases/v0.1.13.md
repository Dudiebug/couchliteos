# MoonlightOS v0.1.13

MoonlightOS v0.1.13 replaces the per-machine images with two ISOs that detect
the hardware at boot: a **general** ISO for Intel, AMD, older NVIDIA cards,
Intel Macs, and VMs, and an **NVIDIA** ISO with NVIDIA's own driver for
GeForce GTX 745/750 and GTX 900 to RTX 40 cards.

## Downloads

| File | Hardware |
|---|---|
| `moonlightos-0.1.13-amd64.iso` | General: Intel and AMD graphics, NVIDIA Kepler and older (including the iMac Late 2013), Intel Macs, VMs |
| `moonlightos-0.1.13-nvidia-amd64.iso` | NVIDIA Maxwell to Ada: GeForce GTX 745/750, GTX 900 to RTX 40, and Maxwell laptop GPUs; falls back to nouveau on other GPUs |
| `SHA256SUMS` | Checksums for both ISOs (`sha256sum -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS`) |

NVIDIA RTX 50 (Blackwell) cards are not supported yet. The iMac Late 2013 no
longer has its own ISO; use the general one.

The NVIDIA ISO contains NVIDIA's proprietary driver and firmware from Debian 13,
and both ISOs contain Broadcom's `wl` Wi-Fi driver. See
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. The general ISO has no proprietary NVIDIA driver.

## New

- **Hardware detection at every start.** MoonlightOS picks the right drivers
  for the PC it is on, so a USB stick moved between PCs starts clean. System
  Diagnostics shows what it chose.
- **NVIDIA ISO.** NVIDIA's 550 driver, with Vulkan and NVIDIA video decoding,
  for GPUs on the driver's supported list. Other NVIDIA GPUs use the open
  nouveau driver. A new **Start MoonlightOS (Basic Graphics)** boot entry
  always uses nouveau.
- **Moonlight on nouveau** starts with software H.264 instead of failing to
  find a hardware decoder. Setting `codec` or `decoder` in `config.ini`
  overrides this.
- **Broadcom Wi-Fi** on both ISOs for the chips that need it (for example the
  iMac's BCM4360); other Broadcom chips keep the open drivers.
- **Apple machines** get temperature sensors, and Apple keyboards default to
  F1-F12.
- **Wider hardware support.** Firmware for Intel, AMD, and NVIDIA graphics,
  Intel SOF and Cirrus audio, common Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Ethernet chips, and
  Intel and AMD CPU microcode, plus hardware video decoding (VA-API) for Intel
  and AMD.

## Known limitations

- Not yet tried on real PCs, including the OptiPlex, the iMac Late 2013,
  NVIDIA's driver on a real card, and Secure Boot.
- Installing to disk from the NVIDIA ISO has not been tried yet.
