# CouchLiteOS v0.2.1

v0.2.1 brings the features that were finished too late for v0.2.0, adds
FIND GAMING PCS and STREAM CHECK, and fixes what three bug-hunt passes found.
This is a **draft**: publish it only after the hardware checklist in
`docs/TESTING.md` passes.

## Downloads

| File | Hardware |
|---|---|
| `couchliteos-0.2.1-amd64.iso` | General: Intel and AMD graphics, NVIDIA Kepler and older (including the iMac Late 2013), Intel Macs, VMs |
| `couchliteos-0.2.1-nvidia-amd64.iso` | NVIDIA GeForce GTX 745/750 and 900 to RTX 40 (Maxwell to Ada); falls back to nouveau on other GPUs |
| `SHA256SUMS` | Checksums for both ISOs |

## New

- **STREAM <PC NAME>** at the top of the home screen: one press wakes the
  gaming PC if needed and starts streaming the app chosen in Settings >
  STREAMING. Shown when one PC is paired, or when a PC is chosen there.
- **FIND GAMING PCS** (Settings > STREAMING > PAIR A / ANOTHER GAMING PC, and
  the setup wizard): lists the Sunshine PCs on the network; pick one and type
  the PIN shown. If none is found it says why (PC off or asleep, Sunshine not
  running, another network) and offers WAKE PC, SEARCH AGAIN, or typing the
  address.
- **STREAM CHECK** (Settings > STREAMING): measures the network path to the
  gaming PC for about 5 seconds and says GOOD, OK, or POOR with what to change
  (Wi-Fi band and signal, a cable, SMOOTHER STREAM). Works with Windows PCs
  that ignore ping.
- **SMOOTHER STREAM** lowers stream quality one step, and **PAIR ANOTHER
  GAMING PC** adds a second PC, both in Settings > STREAMING.
- **SCREEN EDGES** and **TEXT SIZE** in Settings > DISPLAY, for TVs that cut
  off the edges of the picture.
- The TV remote's arrows, OK, and Back reach a running app over HDMI-CEC, and
  the TV goes to standby when CouchLiteOS sleeps, but only if the TV is
  showing CouchLiteOS at the time. **TEST REMOTE BUTTONS** (Settings > TV
  CONTROL) shows which remote buttons reach the box.
- Faster start on an installed system: the boot menu is hidden while boots
  succeed and comes back for 5 s after a failed one.

## Changed

- Sleep: hold Guide / PS for **5 s** (was 3 s). It never sleeps while a game
  has the controller.
- Opening Moonlight by hand wakes the gaming PC first; if the PC does not
  answer, Moonlight still opens and says why. An auto-stream stops instead and
  offers WAKE PC. Home during the wait goes back to the launcher.
- After waking from sleep, auto-stream counts down 15 s, so the TV and the
  controller can come back first.
- SLEEP warns when nothing connected can wake the PC (only its power button
  will).
- Error screens only point at buttons they have; BLUETOOTH SETTINGS is not
  offered on a PC without Bluetooth.

## Fixes

- The first start after upgrading from MoonlightOS crashed on the "what's new"
  screen.
- Setup and PAIR ANOTHER GAMING PC no longer wake the default PC first.
- TRY AGAIN after a failed auto-stream streams to the chosen PC again instead
  of opening Moonlight's PC list.
- Changing SCREEN EDGES or TEXT SIZE several times in a row could stop the
  launcher from coming back (systemd's restart limit); it now applies at the
  next start after 3 changes a minute and returns to Settings > DISPLAY.
- TEXT SIZE LARGER no longer makes the screen too small for the launcher
  (it keeps at least 80x24 characters).
- The first A press after waking no longer puts the box back to sleep, and
  idle sleep is off where nothing can wake the box.
- When a newer release exists, the update notice finds it under the
  project's new and old GitHub names.
- Waking up after holding Guide to sleep shows the main menu instead of
  ACTIVE APPLICATIONS.
- The TV remote's CLEAR and colour buttons work in the launcher while an app
  runs in the background (before, only the arrows, OK, and Back did).
