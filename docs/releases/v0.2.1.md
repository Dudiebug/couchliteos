# CouchLiteOS v0.2.1

v0.2.1 brings the features that were finished too late for v0.2.0, adds
SOFTWARE UPDATE, FIND GAMING PCS, and STREAM CHECK, and fixes many bugs.

## Downloads

| File | Hardware |
|---|---|
| `couchliteos-0.2.1-amd64.iso` | General: Intel and AMD graphics, NVIDIA Kepler and older (including the iMac Late 2013), Intel Macs, VMs |
| `SHA256SUMS` | Checksum for the ISO |

There is no NVIDIA ISO for this release. On a newer NVIDIA card (GTX 745 and up), the general ISO uses the open nouveau driver and Moonlight decodes the video in software.

## New

- **STREAM <PC NAME>** at the top of the home screen: one press wakes the
  gaming PC if needed and starts streaming the app chosen in Settings >
  STREAMING. Shown when one PC is paired, or when a PC is chosen there.
- **FIND GAMING PCS** (Settings > STREAMING > PAIR A / ANOTHER GAMING PC, and
  the setup wizard; the choice is SEARCH THE NETWORK): lists the Sunshine PCs
  on the network, one row per PC; pick one and type the PIN shown. If none is
  found it says why (PC off or asleep, Sunshine not running, another network)
  and offers WAKE PC, SEARCH AGAIN, or typing the address.
- **STREAM CHECK** (Settings > STREAMING): measures the network path to the
  gaming PC for about 5 seconds and says GOOD, OK, or POOR with what to change
  (Wi-Fi band and signal, a cable, SMOOTHER STREAM). Works with Windows PCs
  that ignore ping.
- **SMOOTHER STREAM** lowers stream quality one step, and **PAIR ANOTHER
  GAMING PC** adds a second PC, both in Settings > STREAMING.
- **SCREEN EDGES** and **TEXT SIZE** in Settings > DISPLAY, for TVs that cut
  off the edges of the picture.
- **TV remote in apps.** The TV remote's arrows, OK, and Back reach a running
  app over HDMI-CEC, and the TV goes to standby when CouchLiteOS sleeps, but
  only if the TV is showing CouchLiteOS at the time. **TEST REMOTE BUTTONS**
  (Settings > TV CONTROL) shows which remote buttons reach the box.
- **Faster start on an installed system.** The boot menu is hidden while boots
  succeed and comes back for 5 seconds after a failed one.
- **SOFTWARE UPDATE** (Settings): installed boxes download the newest release,
  check it against the release's `SHA256SUMS`, install it, and restart, keeping
  pairings, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Remote Desktop connections, and settings. There is
  no rollback, and a live USB cannot update itself.

## Changed

- **Sleep** now needs Guide / PS held for **5 seconds** (was 3). It never
  sleeps while a game has the controller.
- **Opening Moonlight wakes the gaming PC first.** If the PC does not answer,
  Moonlight still opens and says why. An auto-stream stops instead and offers
  WAKE PC. Home during the wait goes back to the launcher.
- **Auto-stream after sleep** counts down 15 seconds, so the TV and the
  controller can come back first.
- **SLEEP warns** when nothing connected can wake the PC (only its power button
  will).
- **Error screens** only point at buttons they have; BLUETOOTH SETTINGS is not
  offered on a PC without Bluetooth.

## Fixed

- **Crash after upgrading from MoonlightOS.** The first start crashed on the
  "what's new" screen.
- **Setup and PAIR ANOTHER GAMING PC** no longer wake the default PC first.
- **TRY AGAIN after a failed auto-stream** streams to the chosen PC again
  instead of opening Moonlight's PC list.
- **Changing SCREEN EDGES or TEXT SIZE several times in a row** could stop the
  launcher from coming back. After 3 changes in a minute, the change now
  applies at the next start and you return to Settings > DISPLAY.
- **TEXT SIZE LARGER** no longer makes the screen too small for the launcher
  (it keeps at least 80x24 characters).
- **Waking from sleep.** The first A press after waking no longer puts the box
  back to sleep, and idle sleep is off where nothing can wake the box.
- **Update notice.** When a newer release exists, the notice finds it under the
  project's new and old GitHub names.
- **Main menu after sleep.** Waking up after holding Guide to sleep shows the
  main menu instead of ACTIVE APPLICATIONS.
- **TV remote in the launcher.** The CLEAR and colour buttons work while an app
  runs in the background (before, only the arrows, OK, and Back did).

## Known limitations

- Some Windows PCs may not answer FIND GAMING PCS; typing the address still
  works.

## Updating

This is the first release with SOFTWARE UPDATE. Boxes on 0.2.0 or older must
be reinstalled once; see [Updating](https://github.com/Dudiebug/couchliteos/wiki/Updating)
in the wiki. After that, newer releases come from Settings > SOFTWARE UPDATE.
