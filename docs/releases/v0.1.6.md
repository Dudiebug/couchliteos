# MoonlightOS v0.1.6

MoonlightOS v0.1.6 adds Bluetooth settings you can use with a controller,
without leaving the launcher.

## New

- **Bluetooth in Settings.** Turn Bluetooth on or off, search for devices,
  pair, connect, disconnect, and forget devices.
- **Pairing codes.** Confirm a code, see a passkey, or type a PIN with the
  controller. Searching and pairing can be cancelled and stop on their own
  after a while.
- **Bluetooth controllers** work as soon as they are paired, without a launcher
  restart.
- **Bluetooth audio.** Choose a Bluetooth audio device for sound. HDMI or
  DisplayPort sound is not switched away automatically.
- **Support file** includes Bluetooth adapter and connection status. Pairing
  keys are never included.
- **Bluetooth problems stay contained.** A missing or removed adapter, or a
  Bluetooth service restart, returns you to Settings without restarting the
  launcher or sound.

## Known limitations

- Bluetooth radios, controllers, keyboards, and audio devices have not yet been
  tried on real hardware.
