# CouchLiteOS v0.2.2

v0.2.2 is a one-fix release on top of v0.2.1: sound on the TV over
HDMI/DisplayPort.

## Downloads

| File | Hardware |
|---|---|
| `couchliteos-0.2.2-amd64.iso` | General: Intel and AMD graphics, NVIDIA Kepler and older (including the iMac Late 2013), Intel Macs, VMs |
| `SHA256SUMS` | Checksum for the ISO |

There is no NVIDIA ISO for this release. On a newer NVIDIA card (GTX 745 and up), the general ISO uses the open nouveau driver and Moonlight decodes the video in software.

## Fixed

- **Sound on the TV over HDMI/DisplayPort.** On most desktops (Intel or AMD
  HD Audio, for example Dell OptiPlex PCs), Settings > AUDIO OUTPUT listed only
  the built-in speaker, even though the PC saw the TV. The sound card offers
  the speaker and each HDMI/DP port as separate modes, and the speaker mode was
  always chosen. Now the HDMI/DP mode is chosen whenever a TV with speakers is
  attached, and sound follows the TV again when it is unplugged or plugged back in.
- **AUDIO OUTPUT lists every output**, including ones the sound card can only
  play on after switching mode (the speaker while the TV has the sound, or a
  second HDMI/DP port). Choosing one switches over, and the choice is kept
  after a restart while that output is available.
- **Laptop speakers and HDMI/DP.** Laptops with Intel SOF or AMD ACP audio get
  the ALSA UCM files they need for their speakers and HDMI/DP outputs
  (`alsa-ucm-conf`, `alsa-topology-conf`).
- **Support file.** It now includes `/proc/asound/cards` and the HDMI/DP
  monitor data (`audio/eld/`), and `audio.log` records which HDMI/DP mode was
  chosen.

## Known limitations

- In the support file, the commands run as the launcher user (`wpctl`,
  `vulkaninfo`, `vainfo`) show `setpriv: setresuid failed`. Unchanged from
  v0.2.1; the other files are complete.

## Updating

A box on 0.2.1 gets this release from Settings > SOFTWARE UPDATE. Boxes on
0.2.0 or older must be reinstalled once; see
[Updating](https://github.com/Dudiebug/couchliteos/wiki/Updating) in the wiki.
