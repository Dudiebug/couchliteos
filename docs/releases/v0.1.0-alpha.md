# MoonlightOS v0.1.0-alpha

The first public image of MoonlightOS, a game-streaming appliance for x86_64
PCs. This is an alpha prerelease.

## Downloads

Verify the download before writing it to USB:

```bash
sha256sum --check moonlightos-0.1.0-alpha-amd64.iso.sha256
```

See the repository README and `docs/INSTALL.md` for installation instructions.

## New

- **Debian 13 (Trixie) appliance image** for x86_64 UEFI PCs.
- **Full-screen black-and-white launcher**, with no desktop environment.
- **Moonlight** for streaming from a Sunshine gaming PC, picked from the launcher.
- **chiaki-ng** for PlayStation 4/5 consoles on your network.
- **Keyboard and gamepad navigation** in the launcher.
- **Intel graphics with hardware video decoding**, plus PipeWire and ALSA sound.
- **Wired Ethernet** (IPv4, DHCP) that reconnects after the cable is unplugged.
- **Settings, pairings, logs, and app data are kept** after installation.
- **Apps return to the launcher** when they close or crash.
- **USB/IP** for wheels, HOTAS, and specialty controllers, limited to devices
  you allow. Its network port is blocked unless USB/IP is in use.
- **Optional Tailscale**, set up with a QR code, with diagnostics and Tailscale
  SSH controlled by your tailnet's access rules.
- **Install to an internal SSD** with the Debian installer from the boot menu.

## Known limitations

- Alpha: not yet tried on the target Dell OptiPlex 7010 Micro (DCC36X3).
- 4K60 SDR is best effort; HDR is not supported.
- Wi-Fi and Bluetooth setup are not part of v0.1.
- Tailscale is installed but off and not signed in by default.
- USB/IP shares nothing until you add devices to its allowlist.
- A live USB is for trying MoonlightOS; install to the SSD to keep your data.
