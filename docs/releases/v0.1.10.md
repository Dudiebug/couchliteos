# MoonlightOS v0.1.10

MoonlightOS v0.1.10 improves control of devices and running apps.

## New

- **Cleaner Bluetooth list.** Searching hides unnamed devices and devices shown
  only by their address, but keeps named paired devices.
- **Pick any sound output** in Settings: onboard, HDMI/DisplayPort, USB, or a
  connected Bluetooth device.
- **Protected video in Firefox.** Firefox turns on the standard Widevine DRM
  path, keeps its profile, and shows better diagnostics for protected media.
- **App controls from full screen.** Home on a keyboard, or Guide/Home on a
  supported controller, opens controls for the running apps.
- **Resume or close apps.** Running Moonlight, Chiaki-ng, Firefox, and your own
  apps can be resumed or closed without restarting the launcher.
- **Reorder apps.** In Edit Applications, Left/Right moves an app right away,
  and the order is kept.
- **Support file** includes audio, Bluetooth, Firefox DRM, time, graphics, and
  running-app details, without private data.

## Known limitations

- Disney+ does not currently list Linux among its supported browser setups.
  MoonlightOS offers Firefox's normal Linux Widevine support but does not work
  around streaming services' device rules or DRM.
