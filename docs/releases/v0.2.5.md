# CouchLiteOS v0.2.5

v0.2.5 stops the Super (Windows) key from opening Home, lets you choose the
keyboard shortcut that does, and names keyboard keys in the on-screen help.

## Downloads

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `couchliteos-0.2.5-amd64.iso` | Intel and AMD graphics, NVIDIA Kepler and older (including the iMac Late 2013), Intel Macs, VMs |
| `couchliteos-0.2.5-nvidia-amd64.iso` | NVIDIA GeForce GTX 745/750, GTX 900 series and newer (up to RTX 40) |
| `SHA256SUMS` | Checksums for the ISOs |

## New

- **Choose your own keyboard Home key.** Settings > CONTROLS > KEYBOARD HOME
  KEY: press A or Enter, hold the keys you want, then let go. Ctrl+Alt+H stays
  the default. A key with Ctrl, Alt or Super works anywhere, including during a
  stream or Remote Desktop session. The same screen turns the shortcut off
  (Y / Square, or Delete) or puts Ctrl+Alt+H back (X / Triangle, or F12). Boxes that had the shortcut off keep it off.
- **A KEYBOARD KEYS help page.** Settings > CONTROLS > KEYBOARD KEYS lists what
  each keyboard key does in the launcher: Enter, Esc, the arrow keys, F5 to F8,
  F12 for the on-screen keyboard, and your Home key. CONTROLLER BUTTONS shows
  your Home key too, and the hints at the bottom of screens name Enter and Esc
  where there is room.

## Changed

- **The Super (Windows) key no longer opens Home.** A tap now does nothing in the
  launcher and reaches the app in front, as on any other desktop. Use your
  keyboard Home key (Ctrl+Alt+H unless you changed it), Guide, or the Home
  shortcut on your controller.

## Fixed

- **The first-run setup footer and the APPLICATIONS help** named controller
  buttons only, and one line pointed at the wrong settings screen. They now
  name the keyboard keys and the right place.
- **What's New** named the wrong settings screen for the audio output choice.
  It now says SETTINGS > AUDIO.

## Known limitations

- During a stream or Remote Desktop session your Home key also reaches the other
  PC. Super+letter often does something there (on Windows, Win+H starts voice
  typing); the default, Ctrl+Alt+H, does nothing on Windows.

## Updating

A box on 0.2.1 or newer gets this release from Settings > SOFTWARE UPDATE.
Boxes on 0.2.0 or older must be reinstalled once; see
[Updating](https://github.com/Dudiebug/couchliteos/wiki/Updating) in the wiki.
